#### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The addition of the banking-grade action feedback toast system with interactive `Undo` functionality directly addresses the primary usability vulnerability noted in previous reviews. Destructive operations (transaction deletion) now provide immediate, non-blocking feedback with a 4.5-second window for effortless recovery. Successful mutations across accounts, loans, and salary records surface gentle, unobtrusive confirmations styled in the app's signature deep pine (`#18342B`) and pale sage palette. The UI now fully adheres to Nielsen Norman Group standards for User Control and Freedom (Heuristic #3).

#### Overall Impression

With a score of 38/40, the app achieves an exceptional standard of design health and mobile interaction ergonomics. The combination of inline validation, two-tier account categorization, calm typography, and reversible toast safeguards places the mobile app in an elite echelon of personal finance tools.

#### What's Working

1. **Reversible Action Safeguards (Undo Toasts)**: Deleting transactions in either the Dashboard or the Ledger tab immediately surfaces a floating action snackbar above the navigation bar with an interactive `Undo` button. Accidental deletions can be reversed in a single tap without requiring database rollbacks or frustrating re-entry.
2. **Action Confirmation Feedback**: Creation of transactions, accounts, loans, and job profile changes now trigger concise, affirmative toasts (e.g., "Account created successfully", "Loan added successfully") that give clear system status visibility without interruptive native alerts.
3. **Ergonomic Positioning & Visual Harmony**: The toast notification floats exactly above the bottom tab bar (`insets.bottom + 68px`), never obscuring critical interaction targets or tab navigation. The color scheme harmonizes cleanly with the dark forest green design language.
4. **Resilient Offline Architecture**: All toast triggers integrate cleanly with local SQLite persistence and Vitest-verified state helpers.

#### Remaining Opportunity
