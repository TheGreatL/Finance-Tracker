#### Overall Impression

With a score of 37/40, the app enters the "Excellent" tier. The visual hierarchy is calm, the interaction model is predictable, and error handling is humane and non-disruptive. The app feels like a flagship fintech product designed with craft and intentionality.

#### What's Working

1. **Inline Field Validation**: Replacing generic alert dialogs with targeted inline error states (amount, title, account selection, due dates) in `TransactionFormModal`, `AddLoanModal`, and `AddJobModal` keeps user focus intact and preserves inputs.
2. **Two-Tier Accounts Hierarchy**: The structured separation of liquid accounts from fixed obligations completely eliminates off-screen horizontal scrolling and cognitive overload.
3. **Financial Pulse Integration**: The unified 3-metric strip on the Dashboard surfaces upcoming commitments with direct deep-links to their respective account views.
4. **Cohesive Design Discipline**: 0 emojis, 0 arbitrary font weights, and single-elevation card containers give the entire surface a dependable banking-grade demeanor.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P2] Reversible Action Safeguards (Undo Snackbars)**:
    - **Why it matters**: Deletions in Ledger are immediate upon confirmation; there is no temporary undo toast if a user accidentally confirms.
    - **Fix**: Introduce a temporary undo toast (e.g., "Transaction deleted — Undo") that delays hard deletion by 4 seconds.
    - *_Suggested command_: `$impeccable clarify`
- **[P2] One-Tap Transaction Quick-Presets**:
    - **Why it matters**: Frequent daily micro-expenses (e.g. coffee, commute fare, lunch) require opening the full modal and selecting the pocket each time.
    - **Fix**: Add 2-3 frequent quick-expense chips on the Dashboard Quick Actions bar.
    - *_Suggested command_: `$impeccable delight`
